About Me

I'm someone who has always been pretty independent and motivated to build a life for myself. I moved out of my parents' house at a young age and moved to a completely different city, which taught me a lot about being responsible for myself. I currently live on my own and work two jobs, which keeps me busy but also gives me a good balance. My main job is something I'm really passionate about because it is related to health and wellness, where we sell supplements and help people reach their goals.1 My other job is as a server at a restaurant, which is very different but keeps life fun and social.

I'm also someone who really enjoys having routine and structure in my everyday life. I go to the gym almost every day and especially enjoy lifting weights and working on my strength. Health and nutrition are areas I've become genuinely interested in, and I have completed certifications in nutrition coaching, senior fitness, and strength and conditioning. I enjoy learning more about how the body works and finding ways to improve my own health.2 Outside of work and the gym, I like going on hikes and spending time in nature, especially walking through Gatineau parks. I think staying active is an important part of my lifestyle, but I also enjoy having a good balance and making time for things that make me happy.
